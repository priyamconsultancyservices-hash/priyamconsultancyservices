Buyers judge your website, profile and brand before calling. Many manufacturers lose enquiries silently.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    01Which industries do you work with?+

    We work with businesses in every industry, including manufacturers, traders, service firms, healthcare, education, real estate and startups. We have a strong focus on website design for manufacturing companies and B2B businesses, and we tailor every kit to your industry and buyers.

    02Why do businesses need digital marketing?+

    Digital marketing for manufacturers and other businesses helps customers find you when they search, instead of relying only on referrals, middlemen and portals. Our kit includes SEO, a Google Business Profile and AI search setup, so you show up where buyers look.

    03What should a business website include?+

    It should build trust quickly. For manufacturers, that means product and capability pages, certifications, infrastructure and a downloadable company profile. For service businesses, it means clear services, credentials and easy enquiry options. Either way, it should be more than a good-looking design.

    04What is ChatGPT search visibility?+

    People increasingly ask AI tools like ChatGPT to suggest suppliers and providers. We structure your company information, offerings and web presence so these tools can understand your business and find it more easily. No one can guarantee a ranking, but a clear, well-structured presence improves your chances.

    05When will we see results on Google?+

    A Google Business Profile can start showing in local results within days of verification. SEO for a new website usually takes a few months to build momentum, depending on competition and keywords. We share a clear plan and never promise overnight rankings.

    06Do you work with businesses outside Coimbatore?+

    Yes. We are a website company in Coimbatore, and we work with businesses across India through calls, video meetings and site visits where possible.
