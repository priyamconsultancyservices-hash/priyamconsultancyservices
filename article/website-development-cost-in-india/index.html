Website Development Cost in India (2026): Real Ranges by Type, Not Guesswork

With over 950 million internet users, India is one of the fastest-growing digital markets in the world — and for most businesses, that growth starts with Google search. If you're not ranking, you're simply not being found.

That's what makes "how much does a website cost" such a loaded question. The honest answer depends entirely on what you're building, and most business owners have no real way to know where their project should land.

The truth is, website development cost in India isn't one figure — it depends entirely on what you're building. A brochure site and a full-scale e-commerce platform solve completely different problems, and pricing them the same way is where most businesses get misled. Getting a clear picture of website development rates in India before you request a quote puts you in a much stronger position to compare what you're actually being offered.

What Actually Affects the Cost of a Website Development in India?

Before looking at price tables, it helps to understand what's actually driving the number on your quote. Five factors matter more than anything else:

Website type

A static informational site costs far less than a dynamic, database-driven platform.

Design complexity

Template-based design is cheaper; fully custom UI/UX takes more hours to build.

Platform choice

WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, and fully custom code all carry different licensing, dev, and upkeep costs.

Features & integrations

Payment gateways, CRM sync, booking systems, and third-party APIs each add development time.

Who builds it

A freelancer, a small studio, and a full-service agency price the same scope very differently.

These factors rarely act alone — a custom WooCommerce store with multiple payment gateways, for instance, stacks platform cost, feature complexity, and design work into a single quote, which is exactly why two "e-commerce website" estimates can differ by lakhs even for what looks like the same request on paper.

Once you know which of these apply to your project, the price range narrows quickly — and that's exactly what the next few sections cover. Working with a provider that offers complete Website development services under one roof, rather than piecing together freelancers for each part, is usually where that clarity starts.

If you're specifically comparing WordPress website development cost in India, expect the range to widen based on premium themes, plugin licensing, and whether the design is templated or custom-built from scratch. A templated WordPress build sits at the lower end; a custom WordPress theme with unique layouts, animations, and integrations pushes well past ₹1,50,000.

E-Commerce Website Development: Why Costs Vary So Widely

This is where the cost of e-commerce website development in India varies the most, since "an online store" can mean very different things depending on scale and complexity.

A basic online store built on Shopify or WooCommerce with a limited catalog can launch for ₹15,000 – ₹1,00,000, depending primarily on product count:

  • Up to 15 products: ₹15,000 – ₹30,000
  • 16–30 products: ₹20,000 – ₹40,000
  • 30–50 products: ₹40,000 – ₹75,000
  • Above 50 products: ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000

But a custom-built e-commerce platform with advanced inventory management, multiple payment gateways, and high-traffic scalability can run ₹3,00,000 – ₹15,00,000+. What drives that gap includes:

  • Catalog size and product complexity (variants, bundles, custom attributes)
  • Number of payment gateways and third-party integrations
  • Inventory management and multi-warehouse support
  • Expected traffic volume and scalability requirements

The platform you choose changes the ceiling dramatically — which is exactly why "how much does an e-commerce site cost" never has a single answer. Hidden & Ongoing Costs Most Quotes Don't Include

This is the section most website quotes conveniently skip — and it's often where the real budget surprises show up.

  • Domain and hosting: ₹1,500 – ₹15,000/year, depending on hosting quality and traffic needs.
  • SSL certificate: Often free with hosting, but premium SSL can run ₹2,000 – ₹10,000/year.
  • Maintenance and updates: ₹5,000 – ₹25,000/month for security patches, plugin updates, and backups.
  • Content creation:Copywriting, product photography, and blog content are frequently quoted separately.
  • SEO and marketing: A website without ongoing SEO rarely ranks — budget ₹15,000 – ₹75,000/month if visibility matters to you.
  • Third-party app licenses: Payment gateways, CRM tools, and marketing plugins often carry their own subscription costs.

If a quote you've received looks unusually low compared to everything above, it's worth asking directly what's excluded — not after the contract is signed, but before.

How to Choose the Right Website Development Partner

Price should never be the only filter. Before signing off on any quote, check for:

  • A portfolio in your industry or a comparable one — not just generic templates.
  • Clarity on what's included — design, development, SEO setup, revisions, and support, spelled out in writing.
  • Ownership of your code and assets — you should fully own your website, not be locked into a vendor.
  • Post-launch support terms — what happens when something breaks in month three?
  • Realistic timelines — a five-page business website shouldn't take four months, and a custom platform shouldn't be promised in two weeks.

If you're specifically looking for a website development company in Coimbatore, ask for local case studies and references you can actually verify — proximity matters less than proof of delivery, but working with a team that understands your regional market can still be a meaningful advantage for local SEO and support responsiveness.

Conclusion

Website development cost in India isn't a mystery once you separate it by type, platform, and provider. A brochure site, a WooCommerce store, a Shopify build, and a custom web application all sit on very different points of the pricing spectrum — and the right number for your business depends entirely on what you're trying to achieve, not what a random quote says.

The smartest move isn't chasing the cheapest number. It's understanding exactly what's included, what's missing, and what it will actually cost you to fix later if the foundation isn't right. Compare quotes with that lens, ask the right questions upfront, and you'll invest in a website that pays for itself — instead of one you end up rebuilding within a year.
